Heritage Abuja Explorer

WDD 131 Website Planning Document

Site Name

Heritage Abuja Explorer

This name was chosen because the website focuses specifically on the cultural heritage, historical landmarks, tourist attractions, and unique experiences found in Abuja, Nigeria's capital city. The name reflects the site's purpose of helping visitors and residents discover and appreciate Abuja's rich history and attractions.

Optional domain name: heritageabujaexplorer.com

Site Purpose

Heritage Abuja Explorer is an informational tourism and culture website designed to showcase Abuja's historical landmarks, cultural sites, recreational destinations, and visitor attractions. The site provides educational content, travel information, and recommendations for both tourists and local residents interested in exploring Abuja.

  • Highlight historical and cultural landmarks in Abuja.
  • Provide information about major tourist attractions.
  • Share travel tips and visitor recommendations.
  • Promote appreciation of Abuja's heritage and culture.

Scenarios

  • What are the top historical and cultural attractions to visit in Abuja?
  • Where can I find information about famous landmarks such as Zuma Rock and the National Mosque?
  • What activities and destinations would you recommend for a first-time visitor to Abuja?

Color Schema

#1B4332 (Dark Green)
Used for headings, navigation, footer, and important page elements.
#F4A261 (Warm Orange)
Used for accents, buttons, highlights, borders, and visual emphasis.

Typography

Primary Font: Poppins

  • Headings: Poppins Bold (700)
  • Body Text: Poppins Regular (400)
  • Navigation: Poppins Semi-Bold (600)

Poppins was selected because it provides a clean, modern, and professional appearance while maintaining excellent readability on both desktop and mobile devices.
